THE SIMULATION,
BY THE NUMBERS

Techfest isn't a college fest running in the background — it's the main process. Since 1998, a student-run core team has been compiling a festival that pulls in builders, hackers, and dreamers from every state and a few dozen countries besides. Robowars, drone races, lecture series with ISRO chairmen, and an exhibition floor that's hosted humanoid robots — it all runs on the same kernel: people who'd rather build the future than wait for it.
